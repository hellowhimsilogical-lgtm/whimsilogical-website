The apps

Every Whimsilogical app shares the same promise: no pressure, no guilt, no fighting your own software. Satisfying to finish, gentle when you can't. Here's what's live and what's coming.

Live now

Whimsydo live

A zero-pressure to-do list. Capture what's on your mind, finish what you can, and never feel nagged by what you didn't.
Idea Vault live

A home for every idea. Capture sparks, tag them, and watch unexpected connections surface between them.
SpeakEasy live

An AAC app for augmentative and alternative communication. Tap to talk, your voice your way.

On the horizon

Hearth coming soon

A family organizer that keeps shared calendars, chores, shopping lists, and gift plans in one warm place.

Loki's Whims coming soon

A decision-fatigue app for the days choosing feels impossible. Can't decide? Let Loki pick — one thing at a time.

Recipe Vault coming soon

Your recipes, beautifully kept and easy to find — with shopping lists that sync to the rest of your day.

Stardew Tracker coming soon

Track goals, tasks, and collections across your farm — bundles, fish, artifacts, and more. Solo or co-op.

Menagerie coming soon
working title

Health records for every animal in your care, all in one den — vet visits, meds, weights, and reminders.
