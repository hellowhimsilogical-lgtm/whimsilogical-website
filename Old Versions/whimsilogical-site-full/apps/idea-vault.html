Questions

FAQ

Is Idea Vault free?
Yes, completely free. No account, no subscription.
Where is my data stored?
Everything stays on your device in local storage. Nothing is sent anywhere. Export a backup regularly from Settings.
Can I use it on my phone?
Yes. Open in your browser and use "Add to Home Screen" to install. Works on iOS, Android, and desktop.
