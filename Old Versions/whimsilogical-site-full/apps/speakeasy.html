Questions

FAQ

Is SpeakEasy free?
Yes, completely free. No account, no subscription.
Does it work offline?
Yes. Once installed, SpeakEasy works without an internet connection. Speech synthesis uses the device's built-in voices.
How do I update my phrases or contact info?
Open Settings from within the app. Everything is editable — name, safe person contact, phrases, and categories.
Can I install it on my phone?
Yes. Open in your browser, then use "Add to Home Screen" to install it like an app. Works on iOS and Android.
