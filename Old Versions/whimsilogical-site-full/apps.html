The apps

Every Whimsilogical app shares the same promise: no pressure, no guilt, no fighting your own software. Satisfying to finish, gentle when you can't. Here's what's live and what's coming.

Live now

WhimsyDo Live

A zero-pressure to-do list. Capture what's on your mind, finish what you can, and never feel nagged by what you didn't.
Idea Vault Live

A home for every idea. Capture sparks, tag them, and watch unexpected connections surface between them.
SpeakEasy Live

An AAC app for augmentative and alternative communication. Tap to talk — your voice, your way.

In beta

Hearth Beta

A family organizer that keeps shared calendars, chores, shopping lists, and gift plans in one warm place.
