Hearth

Where your family gathers. The heart of your home.

Hearth is a family organizer that keeps shared calendars, chores, shopping lists, gift tracking, and household announcements in one warm, cozy place. Built for real families — the complicated, wonderful, chaotic kind. Currently in active beta testing.

What's inside

Features

📅

Family calendar

Shared family events with per-event visibility — public to the family, private, or tagged to specific members.

🧹

Chores & maintenance

Assign chores, track house maintenance tasks with due dates and priority, and reassign with a tap.

🛒

Shopping lists

Multiple named lists, creator-controlled visibility, optional calendar event link. Archive when done.

🎁

Gift tracker

Private per-user gift planning. Track ideas, committed gifts, occasions, and spend — hidden from recipients.

📋

Family board

A shared announcement space for the whole household. Pin what matters, archive what's done.

💼

Business tracker

Track household businesses and side projects with status badges and color coding.

⚙️

Settings & customization

Member management, color themes, emoji picker, holiday sets, and profile customization.

Questions

FAQ

Why is it called Hearth?
A hearth is the heart of a home — the warm center where a family gathers. The name reflects the app's purpose: not a productivity tool, but a place that feels like home.
When will it be publicly available?
Hearth is currently in active beta with a small testing group. Follow the Whimsilogical Substack for release announcements.
Will it stay free?
The core app will be free. Pricing for any future premium features hasn't been decided yet.
Is my family's data private?
Yes. Hearth syncs your family's data securely through Supabase. Everything is encrypted in transit. Only family members you invite can access your Hearth.
